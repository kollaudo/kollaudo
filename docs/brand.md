Keep the paper background: the logo must read on both light and dark themes.

## Colors

Ink violet is Kollaudo. The other colors are verdicts, and are used only for results.
Keeping them apart means a green, red or amber mark on screen always says something about a test.

| Role | Color | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Brand | ink violet `#5B3E96` | logo, links, primary buttons, headings |
| Paper | `#F3EEE3` | background of the logo and brand images |
| Passed | green `#1A7F37` | results only |
| Failed | red `#CF222E` | results only |
| Flaky, warning | amber `#9A6700` | results only |
| Skipped, pending, other | gray `#6E7781` | results only |

Surfaces in the UI are neutral grays, in light and dark themes. Dark-theme variants of these colors
are defined with the UI.
